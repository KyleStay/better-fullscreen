Watch video across your current Chrome tab without moving or resizing the browser window.

Better Fullscreen replaces compatible website video fullscreen requests with a player view that fills the browser viewport. It is designed for Chrome on macOS, where you may prefer to keep video in the current window rather than enter a separate fullscreen Space.

CONTROLS
• Use the website player's fullscreen button to fill the current tab.
• Press Escape or click × to return to the page.
• Click the outward-corners icon when you want native fullscreen.
• Click the inward-corners icon to leave native fullscreen.
• Use the extension's toolbar menu to turn replacement off for a particular site.

LOCAL PROCESSING
Player layout and the current site's address are processed in your browser. Site overrides are saved on your device. The extension does not transmit this information to a server and has no analytics, ads or tracking.

COMPATIBILITY
For Chrome on macOS. The extension works with compatible JavaScript Fullscreen API players and permitted embedded frames. Some browser-provided video controls, protected pages and custom players may still use native fullscreen. If a site does not work well, turn replacement off for that site. The extension does not download videos or bypass site access restrictions.

After installing, reload already-open video pages before trying their fullscreen button.
