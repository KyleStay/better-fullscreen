Privacy policy

Better Fullscreen processes the current site's address and video-player layout in your browser to provide its viewport and fullscreen controls.

On-device information

The extension checks the site's hostname to apply your per-site setting and inspects player elements, iframe relationships and styles. Disabled-site hostnames are saved in Chrome's local extension storage. Temporary random session tokens and tab IDs coordinate the media view. It does not keep a browsing-history log, copy video files, capture screenshots, record audio or read passwords or form values.

No uploads or tracking

The extension has no analytics, advertising, accounts or developer-server connections. It does not transmit or sell the current page, its address, media content or your settings. Video services and websites continue their normal network activity under their own policies.

Retention and control

Site overrides remain until you re-enable the extension for that hostname or uninstall it. Temporary state ends with the media view, navigation, tab closure or Chrome clearing session storage. Chrome's site-access controls let you restrict where the extension runs.

Limited use

Locally processed information is used only for displaying video in the current tab and controlling fullscreen. The use of information received through Chrome APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It is not used for advertising, profiling or credit decisions.

Support

Open a GitHub issue for support. Information you voluntarily post there is public and handled by GitHub; the extension does not attach browsing data or logs automatically.
