# Better Fullscreen privacy policy

Better Fullscreen is a Chrome extension for macOS that expands website video players within the current tab and provides an optional native fullscreen control.

## Information used on your device

The extension uses the current tab's web address to identify the site's hostname and apply your per-site setting. It inspects video-player elements, iframe relationships, fullscreen state and styles on permitted pages, and temporarily changes player layout. These operations happen within your browser. It does not copy video files, capture screenshots, record audio, read passwords or inspect form values.

The extension saves the hostnames for which you disable it in Chrome's local extension storage. It also keeps temporary random session tokens associated with browser tab IDs in Chrome's session storage so that frames can coordinate the media view. It does not build a browsing-history log or store the full URLs of the pages you watch.

## Information sent or shared

The extension has no analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts or developer-operated server connections. It does not transmit the page, its address, player content or your settings to the developer or another party, and does not sell or share that information. Websites and video services continue to make their own normal network requests and remain subject to their own privacy policies.

## Retention and control

Per-site overrides stay on this device until you re-enable the extension for that hostname or uninstall it. Session state is cleared when the media view ends, the tab navigates or closes, or Chrome clears the extension's session storage. Uninstalling removes the extension's stored settings. Chrome's site-access controls let you restrict the pages where the extension runs.

## Limited use

Better Fullscreen uses the locally processed information only for its single purpose: displaying website video in the current tab and controlling fullscreen. Its use of information received through Chrome APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or an unrelated purpose.

## Support

For questions, open an issue at [KyleStay/better-fullscreen](https://github.com/KyleStay/better-fullscreen/issues). Information you choose to post there is public and is handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy. The extension does not automatically attach browsing information or diagnostic logs to issues.
